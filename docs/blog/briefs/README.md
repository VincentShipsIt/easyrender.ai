# Briefs

One research brief per guide, `{group}.md`, written at stage 1 of the
[pipeline](../PIPELINE.md) from [`prompts/01-research-brief.md`](../prompts/01-research-brief.md),
with the stage 3 depth-gate verdict appended at the end. The brief is the record of where every
fact and number in a guide came from.

The seven week 1 guides (11 to 17 November 2026) were written before this pipeline existed and
have no brief; their sources are inline in each guide.
