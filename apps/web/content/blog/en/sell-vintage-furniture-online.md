To sell vintage furniture online, pick a platform that fits the piece's value, price it from sold listings, photograph it in daylight from every side including its flaws, and write a listing that names maker, era, material and measured size. Chairish and 1stDibs suit designer pieces. Facebook Marketplace and eBay suit local and lower-priced sales.

## Where should you sell vintage furniture?

Match the platform to the piece. A signed mid-century lounge chair and a 1970s pine dresser have different buyers.

- Chairish: a curated marketplace for vintage and designer furniture, aimed at decorators and design buyers.
- 1stDibs: high-end antiques and design, sold by dealers the platform vets.
- Etsy: accepts items at least 20 years old as vintage, and suits smaller pieces a courier can take.
- eBay: wide reach, plus a record of what buyers actually paid.
- Facebook Marketplace: local buyers who collect, with no shipping to arrange.

Fees, seller approval and delivery rules differ between them and change over time. Read the seller terms before you list your first piece.

## How do you price a vintage piece?

Search sold listings, not asking prices. On eBay, tick Sold Items in the filters to see what buyers paid for the same maker and model.

Condition moves the price more than age. A teak sideboard with its original handles and clean veneer sells for more than the same model with a replaced top.

Look for a maker's label, stamp or tag under drawers, seats and tabletops. A name like Ercol or G-Plan on a British piece changes who searches for it, and what they search for.

## How do you photograph vintage furniture?

Shoot in daylight, against a plain wall, with the camera at the middle height of the piece. Take the full set: front, three-quarter, side, back, and the inside of every drawer and cupboard.

Then photograph every flaw close up: a water ring on the top, a chip in the veneer, a joint that moves. Vintage buyers expect wear. What they object to is wear they were not shown.

The light and the setup are covered in [product photography for ecommerce](/learn/product-photography-for-ecommerce).

## What should the listing say?

Lead with the words buyers type: maker, style, material and object. "Ercol elm Windsor dining chair, 1960s" finds more buyers than "Lovely old chair".

Give dimensions you have measured with a tape: width, depth and height, plus seat height for chairs and inside depth for shelves. Buyers measure their alcove before they message you.

Describe condition plainly, with the same flaws you photographed. "Light surface scratches on top, small chip on back left leg, see photo 7" reads as honest and heads off disputes.

## Can a 3D model help sell a one-off piece?

A vintage piece has no catalogue shoot and no second unit. You have it once, so you photograph it once, and a buyer two hundred miles away has to judge it from those pictures.

A 3D model made from one good photo lets that buyer turn the piece round on screen. Most marketplaces do not accept 3D files in a listing, so in practice you send a link when someone asks for more angles, or add the GLB to your own Shopify store as product media.

easyrender gives each finished model a page you can share by link. Today that link only works while the model is in the public gallery. The model shows shape and materials, not condition or size, so the close-ups and your tape measurements stay in the listing.

## How do you ship vintage furniture?

Decide before you list. Collection only keeps things simple but limits you to local buyers. Offering delivery widens the market and means getting a quote before the sale.

For a courier, wrap the piece in moving blankets, take off legs and glass where you can, and tape the screws in a labelled bag inside a drawer. Photograph it just before it leaves, in case of a damage claim.

## FAQ

### What is the best site to sell vintage furniture?

Designer and signed pieces do best on curated sites like Chairish or 1stDibs, where buyers expect to pay for provenance. Everyday vintage, like a pine chest of drawers, sells faster on Facebook Marketplace or eBay. Small items that ship by courier suit Etsy.

### How old does furniture have to be to count as vintage?

There is no single legal rule, but Etsy's threshold is a common reference: an item has to be at least 20 years old to be listed as vintage. Antique usually means 100 years or older, the age US Customs uses for duty-free antiques. Give the decade in the listing rather than relying on the word.

### Do you need professional photos to sell vintage furniture?

No, a phone on a tripod, daylight from a window and a plain wall are enough. What matters is a complete set of angles and honest close-ups of every flaw. Buyers trust clear, plain photos of the real piece more than styled shots that hide the back and the wear.

### Should you include measurements in the listing?

Yes, always, measured by you with a tape: width, depth and height, plus seat height for chairs. Buyers check them against their space before they ask a question. Photos and 3D models do not carry reliable measurements, so the numbers in the text are what the buyer relies on.
